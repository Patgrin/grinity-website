Delete your data

for the game "Grumpy Scampi" by Grinity e.U.

Grumpy Scampi stores data on our server only if you use the optional online leaderboard. You can delete this data yourself at any time.

How to delete your data in the game

  1. Open Grumpy Scampi.
  2. In the main menu, tap the BEST box ("LEADERBOARD ›").
  3. Tap PROFILE at the top right.
  4. At the bottom, tap DELETE MY LEADERBOARD DATA and confirm with DELETE.

The deletion happens immediately and permanently.

Or by email

Write to contact@grinity.eu with the subject "Grumpy Scampi: delete my data" and include your nickname and friend code (found under Leaderboard → Profile). We delete the data within 30 days and confirm it to you.

What gets deleted

  • Your nickname and friend code
  • Your best results in all worlds (all time and weekly leaderboards)
  • Your friends list and your entries in other players' friends lists
  • Your anonymous player ID

Nothing is kept: after deletion, none of your leaderboard data remains on the server. Technical connection logs of our service provider (such as IP addresses) are deleted there automatically after a short time and are not linked to the leaderboard.

Your progress on your device

Your game progress (coins, pearls, stars, skins etc.) is stored only on your device, not with us. It stays when you delete your leaderboard data. You delete it by uninstalling the app or choosing "Clear data" in the Android settings under Apps → Grumpy Scampi → Storage.
