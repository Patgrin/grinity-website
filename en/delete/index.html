Delete your data

for the game "Grumpy Scampi" by Grinity e.U.

Grumpy Scampi stores data on our server only if you use the optional online features (leaderboard, profile, friends, chat; all only after you choose a nickname). You can delete this data yourself at any time.

How to delete your data in the game

  1. Open Grumpy Scampi.
  2. In the main menu, tap the field with your nickname below the logo (your profile).
  3. Tap the red DATA tile ("delete from server").
  4. Confirm with DELETE.

The deletion happens immediately and permanently.

Or by email

Write to contact@grinity.eu with the subject "Grumpy Scampi: delete my data" and include your nickname and friend code (found in your profile: tap your nickname). We delete the data within 30 days and confirm it to you.

What gets deleted

  • Your nickname and friend code
  • Your best results in all worlds (all time and weekly leaderboards)
  • Your profile (profile card, title, achievement points, stars, skins)
  • Your friends list, friend requests and your entries in other players' friends lists
  • Your chat messages, notices and your block list
  • Your anonymous player ID

What remains: reports you already made about other players stay without any link to your ID and are deleted automatically after 12 months at the latest. Apart from that, none of your online data remains on the server. Technical connection logs of our service provider (such as IP addresses) are deleted there automatically after a short time and are not linked to the leaderboard.

Your progress on your device

Your game progress (coins, pearls, stars, skins etc.) is stored only on your device, not with us. It stays when you delete your online data. You delete it by uninstalling the app or choosing "Clear data" in the Android settings under Apps → Grumpy Scampi → Storage.
