Support

for the game “Grumpy Scampi” by Grinity e.U.

Got a question, found a bug or have an idea? Write to us at contact@grinity.eu – ideally with your device (e.g. iPhone 15, Galaxy S24) and the game version (shown in OPTIONS). We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If you delete the app or reset its data, your progress is gone too. You don't need an account.

I bought something but it is missing in the game.

Open OPTIONS and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. If that doesn't help, write to us with the receipt from the App Store or Google Play.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: for the App Store at reportaproblem.apple.com, for Google Play in the order history of your Google account.

How do I delete my leaderboard data?

Right in the game via the leaderboard, PROFILE. Instructions are on Delete data.

How do I change language, sound or controls?

Tap OPTIONS at the bottom right of the main menu: there you'll find the language (English/German), the volume for sound and music, and the controls (both sides, one-handed left/right, or tilt).

Do I need internet?

No. Only the optional online leaderboard and purchases need a connection.
