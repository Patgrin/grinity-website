Creator Program

for the game “Grumpy Scampi” by Grinity e.U.

Do you show Grumpy Scampi on YouTube, TikTok, Twitch or Instagram? Then apply for your own creator code.

How it works

  • Your fans enter your code in the game: SHOP → ENTER CREATOR CODE.
  • For 14 days they get +15 % pearls and coins on every shop purchase. The price stays the same.
  • You get a share of the revenue made through your code.
  • In the game your fans see “Supporting: your name”.

Apply

Anyone can apply. Write to contact@grinity.eu with the subject “Creator Program” and send us:

  • the link to your channel or channels,
  • the platform and roughly your number of followers,
  • your desired code (3–20 characters, letters and digits, e.g. SCAMPIFAN).

Guide values (not a requirement, smaller channels are welcome to apply): YouTube from approx. 500 subscribers, TikTok from approx. 1,000 followers, Twitch from approx. 100 followers with regular streams, Instagram from approx. 1,000 followers.

We review every application individually and decide at our own discretion whether to give out a code. There is no right to be accepted, and we do not have to give reasons for a rejection. We usually reply within a few days.

Terms of participation

1. Activity
You show Grumpy Scampi at least once every 14 days in a post, video, clip or stream. If no post can be seen for 28 days, we pause your code. New players can then no longer enter it. As soon as you are active again, we will reactivate it on request.

2. Tagging
You mention your code in every post with Grumpy Scampi. On Instagram you also tag @grumpyscampi_thegame or mention it in the caption. On the other platforms you write “Grumpy Scampi” in the title or description. This lets us find your posts and confirm your activity.

3. Labelling as advertising
You label posts with your code as advertising (“Ad”). Also use the platform’s own advertising label if there is one.

4. Not allowed

  • content that glorifies violence, insults, discriminates, is sexual or is otherwise unsuitable for children, if Grumpy Scampi or your code appears in it
  • pressuring children or young people to buy
  • misleading promises such as “free pearls”, “hack” or “cheat”, and advertising cheats, mods or unofficial versions
  • bought followers, bots or artificially generated views
  • purchases with your own code only to increase your share, or purchases made with the aim of having them refunded later
  • spreading your code unasked in other people’s channels, comments or chats (spam)
  • claiming to be an employee of Grinity or speaking on behalf of Grinity
  • breaking the rules of the platform or applicable law

5. Your code
Your desired code must not contain third-party trademarks, insults or anything misleading. We may suggest a different code.

6. Revenue share
We agree on the amount of your share by e-mail. It is based on the revenue from purchases with your code after deducting the fees of Apple and Google, VAT and refunds.

7. Payout
We pay monthly: we transfer the share for a month by the 15th of the month after next (e.g. October by 15 December). If the amount is below €20, it is carried over to the next month. You are responsible for paying tax on your income yourself.

8. Refunds
If a purchase is refunded after we have already paid out the share for it, we deduct this amount from your next payout. If your participation in the Creator Program ends and there are no further payouts, we reserve the right to reclaim these amounts.

9. Under 18
If you are not yet of age, we need the consent of your parents or legal guardians.

10. Blocking and ending
If you break these terms, we can block your code at any time and without warning. In case of serious violations, the share for the affected period is forfeited. Either side can end the participation at any time by e-mail. The share up to the end is then still settled according to sections 7 and 8.

11. Changes
We may change these terms. We inform active creators by e-mail at least 14 days in advance.
