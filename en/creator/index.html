Creator Program

for the game “Grumpy Scampi” by Grinity e.U.

Do you show Grumpy Scampi on YouTube, TikTok, Twitch or Instagram? Then get your own creator code.

How it works

  • Your fans enter your code in the game: SHOP → ENTER CREATOR CODE.
  • For 14 days they get +15 % pearls and coins on every shop purchase. The price stays the same.
  • You get a share of the revenue made through your code. We agree on the share and the payout by e-mail.
  • In the game your fans see “Supporting: your name”.

What we ask for

  • You show Grumpy Scampi regularly, for example in videos, streams or clips.
  • Your content is family-friendly, because children play Grumpy Scampi too.
  • You label posts with your code as advertising (“Ad”), as the law requires.

Apply

Write to contact@grinity.eu with the subject “Creator Program”. Please send us:

  • the link to your channel,
  • the platform and roughly your number of followers,
  • your desired code (3–20 characters, letters and digits, e.g. SCAMPIFAN).

We usually reply within a few days. There is no right to a code; codes can be blocked if the conditions are not met.
