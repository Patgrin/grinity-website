Privacy Policy

for the game "Grumpy Scampi" (Android app, iOS app and browser version). Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:
Grinity e.U. (registered sole proprietorship, company register no. FN 609103s), owner Patrick Limper
Brucknerstraße 7, 4690 Schwanenstadt, Austria
Email: contact@grinity.eu

2. In short

Grumpy Scampi contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services, and needs no account. Your game progress stays on your device. Only if you choose to use the online features (leaderboard, profile, friends, chat; all only with a nickname you pick) are your nickname, records and other game data you share stored on a server (sections 5 and 6). If you make in-app purchases in the app, the service RevenueCat handles them (section 7).

3. Game progress

Your game progress (for example coins, pearls, stars, records, settings such as language, sound and one-handed mode, purchased skins and accessories) is stored only locally on your device in the app storage (localStorage). We have no access to it. This data never leaves your device, except for the online data described in sections 5 and 6.

4. Deleting your game progress

You can delete the locally stored progress at any time: in the Android settings under Apps → Grumpy Scampi → Storage → "Clear data", by uninstalling the app, or in the browser by clearing the site data. Your progress cannot be restored afterwards. Data on the server (leaderboard, profile, friends, messages) is deleted with the button in the game (see section 5).

5. Online leaderboard (optional)

The game offers an online leaderboard with the best heights in each world's endless mode, best results in the minigames (all time and the current week) and a friends leaderboard. Using it is optional. The game connects to the server only when you use an online feature; without a nickname, no results are sent.

What data: a random, anonymous player ID (no name, email or phone number), the nickname you choose, your friend code, your best results per world, minigame and week with their time, the time of your last submission, and the profile data described in section 6. When connecting, the server technically processes your IP address (for the connection and to prevent abuse); it is not linked to the leaderboard.

Publicly visible: all players can see nicknames and best results in the leaderboard; your profile (section 6) can be opened from there. Please do not use your real name as a nickname.

Purpose and legal basis: providing the online features you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); processing the IP address for the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in secure operation).

Service provider: the online features run on Supabase, Inc. (USA) as a processor; the servers are located in Frankfurt am Main (EU). A data processing agreement is in place with Supabase. Access from the USA cannot be entirely ruled out; it is safeguarded by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention and deletion: the data is kept until you delete it. In the game under Profile (the field with your nickname in the main menu) → DATA ("delete from server") you immediately and permanently remove your nickname, results, profile, friendships and friend requests, block list, messages and notices, and the anonymous ID. Exception: reports you have already submitted (section 6) remain, without a link to your ID, for at most 12 months. Alternatively, just email us (see section 14).

6. Profile, friends, chat, reporting (optional, only with a nickname)

If you set a nickname, you can also use these features:

  • Public profile: other players can see your profile: profile card (background, figure with skin and accessories, effect), nickname, active title, achievements and achievement points, best results, collected stars and owned skins. Profile data is sent to the server when it changes.
  • Friends: you send friend requests using the other player's friend code; a friendship exists only once both sides agree. The game shows your own code only when you ask for it.
  • Chat: messages can only be written between confirmed friends (at most 300 characters). A server-side word filter checks them. Messages and notices are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Reporting and blocking: you can report profiles and chat messages. A report stores the reported nickname, for messages their text, your details and your ID; we review reports manually and can exclude players from chat. Reports are deleted automatically after 12 months at the latest. Blocked players can no longer write to you; the block list is stored on the server.
  • Inbox: this shows friend requests, chats, notices and news from the developer ("Grumpy News", the same for everyone, not linked to you personally).

Purpose and legal basis: providing the social features you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); moderation, protecting other players and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Service provider, retention and deletion: as in section 5 (Supabase, Frankfurt; delete via DATEN in the profile or by email).

7. In-app purchases (app only)

In the Android and iOS apps you can buy pearl packs, the starter pack and season passes. The payment itself is handled solely by Google (Google Play) or Apple (App Store); we do not receive payment details (card number etc.). To verify and unlock purchases and to restore earlier purchases, we use the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) as a processor.

What data: an anonymous app user ID from RevenueCat (no name, no email), purchase receipts and transactions (purchased products, time, transaction ID), the store (Apple/Google) and, for technical reasons, device and country information and the IP address. There are no real purchases in the browser; nothing is sent to RevenueCat there.

Creator codes: If you choose to enter a creator code in the shop, only this code is sent to our server (Supabase, Frankfurt, see section 5) to check whether it exists; no player is created and we do not store who entered the code. The code stays stored on your device until it expires after 14 days or you remove it. For purchases in the app we pass the code to RevenueCat as an attribute so that we can give the creator a share of the revenue. Creators do not learn who used their code.

Purpose and legal basis: processing, unlocking and restoring your purchases (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Third country: RevenueCat is based in the USA. The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. More in RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

8. Reminders (local notifications)

The app can notify you with reminders (for example when the daily lucky wheel is ready). These notifications are scheduled and shown only on your device: there is no server, no push token and no data transfer. The app asks for your permission first; you can turn the reminders off at any time in the game's OPTIONS or in your device's system settings.

9. Internet connection

No internet connection is needed to play. All content is included in the game. A connection is made only for the online features (sections 5 and 6) and for in-app purchases (section 7).

10. No sharing, no ads, no trackers

We do not sell or share data with third parties (except the service providers for the online features, section 5, and for in-app purchases, section 7). The app includes no ad networks, analytics tools or trackers. The app does not request special permissions (such as location, contacts, camera or microphone); it only asks for permission to show notifications for reminders (section 8).

11. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Chat is only possible between confirmed friends (both must accept the friendship); messages are filtered, and players can be reported and blocked. Profiles show only nickname, game progress and the chosen figure; nicknames are checked for inappropriate words. Please do not share personal information in chats.

12. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; in Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde (Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at). You can change (nickname, profile) and delete your online data yourself in the game; for anything else, write to us. You delete your local progress yourself (section 4).

13. Changes

If new features that process data are added, we will update this policy beforehand. The current version is always available on this page.

14. Contact

For questions about privacy, please write to contact@grinity.eu.
