Grumpy Scampi

Grumpy Scampi is a free platformer and jumping game for iPhone, iPad and Android. An octopus flings the grumpy crustacean Scampi upwards, and from there he bounces from sea creature to sea creature through six underwater worlds with 60 levels, an endless mode and five minigames.

A scampi in a bad mood. An octopus with way too much power in its tentacle. One swing, and he's airborne. How high he gets is up to you.

Steer Scampi with two arrow buttons, stomp enemies, collect coins and pearls, and battle through six worlds with 60 levels – each with its own twist.

Six worlds

  • Seabed

    Corals, dolphins and clams: this is where Scampi learns to bounce.

  • Deep Sea

    Pitch dark. Only glowing creatures give a little light.

  • Frozen Sea

    Slippery ice floes – if you are not careful, you slide off.

  • Shipwreck

    Cannonballs fly across the screen. Dodge them or jump on them!

  • Volcano

    Bubbling vents shoot Scampi sky-high.

  • Christmas Reef

    Christmas tree worms blink to the beat. Only a well-timed landing gets you further.

Playground: five minigames

  • Long Throw

    The octopus flings Scampi as far as possible – with jellyfish trampolines and turtle turbo.

  • Clam Grab

    Which clam hides the pearl? Watch closely while they shuffle!

  • Octo Bowling

    Scampi is the ball, seahorses are the pins. Strike?

  • Whack-a-Crab

    Whack the enemies, spare your friends – and don't miss the golden crab.

  • Jelly Trampoline

    Place jellyfish under Scampi and send him ever higher.

Frequently asked questions about Grumpy Scampi

What is Grumpy Scampi?

Grumpy Scampi is a free platformer and jumping game for iPhone, iPad and Android by Grinity. An octopus flings the grumpy crustacean Scampi upwards, and from there he bounces from sea creature to sea creature through six underwater worlds with 60 levels in total.

Is Grumpy Scampi free?

Yes. Grumpy Scampi is free to play and contains no ads. There are optional in-app purchases for pearls, a starter pack and a season pass. All worlds and levels can be reached without buying anything.

Which devices does Grumpy Scampi run on?

Grumpy Scampi is coming to iPhone and iPad on the App Store and to Android phones and tablets on Google Play.

When will Grumpy Scampi be released?

Grumpy Scampi will be released on 24 November 2026. The first season, with a Christmas theme, starts on 1 December 2026.

Can you play Grumpy Scampi offline?

Yes. The game needs no internet connection. Only the optional online leaderboard, the social features and purchases need internet.

Do you need an account for Grumpy Scampi?

No. Your progress is saved on your device. For the online leaderboard you only choose a nickname if you want to, with no e-mail address or account.

Which languages is Grumpy Scampi available in?

Grumpy Scampi is available in English and German.

Who developed Grumpy Scampi?

Grumpy Scampi is developed by Grinity e.U., an independent game studio from Austria. Are you a streamer or creator? Take a look at our Creator Program.
