We are a small indie game studio from Schwanenstadt in Upper Austria, making games with character.

Grumpy Scampi

Scampi is a crustacean. Scampi is grumpy. And Scampi gets flung into the air by an octopus. It is not meant kindly, but it helps: from there he bounces from sea creature to sea creature, higher and higher.

Steer Scampi with two arrow buttons, stomp enemies, collect coins and pearls, and explore three worlds with 30 levels: Seabed, Deep Sea and Frozen Sea. Plus a minigame, a wardrobe, daily tasks and a season.

Playable without internet, with no ads and no account. If you like, compete with the world or your friends on the online leaderboard. German and English.
