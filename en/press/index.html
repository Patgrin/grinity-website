Description

Short

Grumpy Scampi is a free platformer for iPhone, iPad and Android. An octopus flings the grumpy crustacean Scampi into the air, and from there he bounces from sea creature to sea creature through six underwater worlds with 60 levels, an endless mode and five minigames. No ads, playable offline.

Long

Scampi is in a bad mood, pretty much always. The octopus doesn't care: it grabs him and hurls him upwards with full force. Since then, Scampi has been bouncing from jellyfish to turtle to dolphin, grumbling his way higher and higher.

Players steer Scampi with two arrow buttons, in one-handed mode or by tilting the device. Jellyfish, clams and turtles are springboards, spiky enemies are dodged or stomped flat, coins and pearls are collected along the way. Each of the six worlds brings its own mechanic: darkness in the Deep Sea, slippery ice floes in the Frozen Sea, cannonballs in the Shipwreck, bubble columns in the Volcano and platforms blinking to the beat in the Christmas Reef. Every world has ten levels with up to three stars and an endless mode for high scores.

Beyond the levels, five minigames await in the Playground, along with a wardrobe of skins and accessories for Scampi (who can also be played as Scampina) and the octopus, achievements and titles, daily and weekly tasks, a daily lucky wheel and a season pass. The first season, with a Christmas theme, starts on 1 December 2026.

Key features

  • Free and without ads, all worlds and levels reachable without purchases
  • Fully playable offline, no account needed
  • Six worlds, each with its own game mechanic
  • Five minigames: Long Throw, Clam Grab, Octo Bowling, Whack-a-Crab, Jelly Trampoline
  • Play as Scampi or Scampina
  • Optional online leaderboard with weekly rankings and friends, nickname only
  • Chest odds openly shown in the game

About Grinity

Grinity e.U. is an independent game studio from Austria developing mobile games for iOS and Android. Its focus is on original characters, a distinctive art style and a fair free-to-play model without ads. Grumpy Scampi is the studio’s first game.
